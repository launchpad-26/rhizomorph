light — pending ruling, illustrative only

The 9b band is luminance-directional and inverts on light ground — “brighter than the calm world” cannot mean the same thing on white. Severity here is carried by enclosure, weight and saturation, plus a per-theme re-derived band. Canvas gains a theme-aware palette path; the colour mirror test doubles. The switch is chrome, not status: persisted preference, defaulting to prefers-color-scheme.

the ambient layerowned by the scene PRD

MAY vary — depth washes, iridescence, texture, environmental tint. Granted through the variation-table device: only channels that carry nothing, each grant bounded, breathing only inside the ambient class (4–8s, ≤3%).

status hue = meaning · never ambient

NEVER — the six semantic hues, the alarm grammar, the salience band, the tissue fence. The accompanying law is four words: ambient never means. “No new hue” survives as “no new semantic hue” — stronger about meaning, not weaker about colour.

Vibrancy rises only via the lawful recipe: chroma (ACTIVITY_TINT) and the floor (CALM_BODY_FLOOR) — the 0.78/0.84 band does not move.
