# The engraved mass — Plate stage 1 (exhibition II's first bounded slice)

Landed 2026-08-21 (professionalisation loop 23). Cited from `marks/root.ts`'s
`STIPPLE`/`stippleMark`, the `mass-stipple` role in `marks/types.ts`, and the
ink exception in `gl/frame.ts`'s `isLight`.

## The election

The Further Departure exhibition deferred Plate (II) — light mode as a
botanical engraving — "unless loops reach them with budget". Loop 23 reached
it. Stage 1 is deliberately one mark family: the mass's interior, on paper
only. Strand hatching, specimen labels and leader lines remain unelected
future stages, each its own bounded slice.

## What it is

On the void, eighteen translucent shells accumulate into depth. On paper the
same accumulation reads as a smudge — engraving is how print carries a
volume. So the LIGHT world alone gets a stipple: up to 460 seeded candidate
points over the disc, each surviving only where the body's own scalar field
(`fieldAt`, the same falloffs the silhouette is walked from) is deeper than
the candidate's seeded threshold. Density IS depth — the texture can never
disagree with the shape, because they are the same function. Dots grade
0.9–2.6px and quiet plum ink by depth; the wash beneath stays.

Texture never means: the dots move nothing, count nothing, and answer no
lane fact. The mass's size and swells carry the meaning they always did; the
stipple redistributes with the field exactly because it belongs to the body.

## The finding that shaped the painter

`isLight()` treated every `motes` mark as light — correct for every drift
that existed (a homecoming glows), and exactly wrong for an engraving:
additive compositing can only lighten, so a dark dot on paper vanishes by
arithmetic. That is how the first three tunings produced nothing at all. The
`mass-stipple` role is the painter's one ink-motes exception, pinned by law.

## Laws (marks.test.ts)

Dark carries the role never (byte-identity by absence); light carries exactly
one mark, bounded, every dot inside 1.06·radius; deterministic per frame;
ink-not-light via the now-exported `isLight`.

## Tuning history, honestly

340 candidates at 0.55–1.05px / α .10–.24 read as a tint, not a texture; the
sign of the field (negative INSIDE — it is a distance) cost one round; the
additive blend cost two more. Landed at 460 / 1.5–2.6px / α .45–.72 with a
0.055 floor. The operator's knobs are all in `STIPPLE`.

## Stage 2 — the specimen leaders (loop 24, same day)

An engraving CONNECTS its labels. On paper every drawn name gains a 0.7px
hairline from just off the node to just short of the text, in the structure
register (`register.unknown` — a leader is apparatus, not matter, and never a
status word). It rides every gate `labelMarks` already has (the label policy,
a hidden retiree, the growth arrive window), so a leader can never point at
an unnamed lane — held as a law, along with dark-absence and
collinearity-with-the-anchor-axis. The dark world keeps its floating names:
in the void, proximity does the connecting.
