# The fruiting material — landed matter's own family (prd-33 amendment)

Landed 2026-08-21 (professionalisation loops 8–9). Cited from
`scene/palette.ts`'s `FRUIT_RAMP`, `retire.ts`'s `persistInks`, the seal in
`marks/node.ts`, the rings in `marks/root.ts`, and the homecoming branch in
`marks/dissolve.ts` / `motes.ts`.

## Why material, not status — the arithmetic

The operator asked for magenta as the colour of landed lanes. As a STATUS hue
it is impossible without demolishing two shipped laws: broken exclusivity
(> 30° from H 13.9) and the accent's clearance (> 60° from H 295.5) jointly
leave only **43.9°–235.5°** — yellow through green, cyan, blue — open to any
status hue, at every angle. The audit that produced those numbers is in the
loop journal; the laws are `palette.test.ts`'s.

So the magenta enters the way the tissue did: as a MATERIAL. Landed matter —
what a lane becomes when its work comes home — fruits. The persist strand and
glyphs, the persisted seal, the heart's growth rings (now genuinely a
spore-print), and a landing's homecoming motes wear the fruiting family. A
dead lane's matter still composts to tissue: death and landing part ways at
the material, deliberately. Law 9a is untouched because the family never
joins the status vocabulary — and the ledger's LANDED chip stays done-green
for exactly that reason.

## The family

Dark (H ≈ 335): `#7e4a72` deep · `#b26ba3` accent · `#d69ac4` light.
Paper (H ≈ 340): `#6d2b58` · `#96407c` · `#c08bb0`. Ground-ward first, like
the tissue ramp; `fruitAtOn()` is `tissueAtOn`'s twin. Measured with
`theme/oklch.ts`: ≥ 33° from broken and ≥ 39° from the accent in both
worlds — and, the trap the audit named in advance, the paper steps sit
**≥ 24 rgb-units from every paper register step**, twice the fence epsilon,
so the light theme's structure ink can never read as fruit.

## The fences, and the partition

The fruiting fence landed BEFORE the first consumer: fruiting ink
(rgb-distance ≤ 12 to the frame palette's own steps) may appear only on
returned-matter roles (`persist`, `persist-bloom`, `persist-mark`,
`growth-ring`, `dissolution`, `absorption`, `homeward`) and never as text or
chip. The tissue fence's violet heuristic (`b > r > g`) fires on the
ICE-fruit mixes the persist recipe produces, so **the two materials partition
by role**: the tissue law polices every non-fruiting role, and the fruiting
roles are held instead by the fruit fence plus the byte pins
(`retire.test.ts`'s recipe≡constant equality, `marks.test.ts`'s endpoint
pins). A violet smuggled onto a persist glyph breaks those pins before it
breaks any heuristic.

## The recipe

`persistInks` keeps its k (0.18) and its alphas; only the family changed. The
fruit-based strand sits at luminance 0.1266 — UNDER `PERSIST_LUMINANCE` 0.14
with more headroom than the green it replaced (0.1388). Every luminance law
(`PERSIST_FLOOR`, the living ≥ CALM_FLOOR > PERSIST hierarchy) re-verified
against the new bytes. `RING_GREEN 0.22` became `RING_FRUIT 0.3` — the
spore-print is the memoir the amendment exists for, so it earns a little
more of its own material.

## Refused, in writing

- **Rim flora fruiting near landed seats** — an ambient channel would then
  correlate with a lane fact, which is exactly what "ambient never means"
  forbids. The flora stays violet.
- **A magenta DOM chip** — a fifth status hue by the back door, breaking the
  hue-gap laws every other surface is sworn on. Words are the DOM's channel
  ("landed"); matter is the scene's.
- **Cause-keyed fruiting** (`DissolutionCause`) — a landing's cord return is
  'severance'-caused, so cause is the wrong key; the split is the lane's own
  declared activity (`done` = landed → fruit).

The at-rest quietness is lawful and deliberate: the persist strand must sit
under `CALM_FLOOR`. The fruit's loud moments are the landing itself (the
homecoming motes) and the rings accumulating in the heart. `RING_FRUIT` is
the knob if the operator wants the memoir louder.
